Why

    What the three answers mean. Prohibited: a rule Shootable checks bans shooting there, for this firearm, today. No prohibition found: none of the rules it checks bans it; rules it doesn't cover, and things no map shows, can still apply. Not confirmed: it can't be settled from the maps, or the data isn't complete or current there.

    A summary of public rules, not a permit or advice on the law. On private land you need the owner's permission. Confirm your target, your backstop and what's beyond it.

    The spot is in this page's address after the #, which your browser keeps to itself: it isn't sent to this site, and this page stores nothing.
